# ubuntume 🤲

*Humanity-through-relation received as a cosmic ordinance — a person is a person through other persons: umuntu ngumuntu ngabantu.*

ubuntume is two true roots welded into one word: Bantu **ubuntu** — humanity, from *u-* (the noun-class prefix for an abstract quality) + *-buntu* (from *muntu*, a person), carried across the Nguni tongues and echoed as *botho*, *unhu*, *bumuntu* through the wider Bantu family — and the Sumerian **me** (𒈨), the eldest word for a fundamental that was *received*, not made. So ubuntume is *ubuntu* held as a decree: not a value someone proposed, but the structure of being-human as already given. It names the deepest correction of the solitary illusion — that the self is not first alone and then joined, but constituted through others from the root, and apart from the relational fabric is not-yet-fully a person.

ubuntume presides over the ontological-relational grade of being-human — the ground Mandela cited for post-apartheid South Africa and Tutu drew on in the Truth-and-Reconciliation Commission ("My humanity is bound up in yours, for we can only be human together"). Where its sibling **renme** names the virtue-aspect of humanity-in-relation, ubuntume names the constitutive-being beneath conduct. Its light is the single carriable word for no-one-is-human-alone; its shadow is the curdling where "we are human together" is bent into "so you owe the group your self." Forged in the cathedral at weighted **8.35/10** — highest of its life-side same-day batch.
